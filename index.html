From the boarding ground to the berth, every checklist item, every leg and every signature is captured digitally, stored securely, and printed into one record. All of the below is ready today, in the browser and in the native iPad and iPhone app.

The exchange & the record

The Master Pilot Exchange

A full A.960 / SOLAS Chapter V checklist, and a form builder, so your organisation runs its form, not ours. Photograph your existing paper form and it's read in for you.

Signed, exportable record

Master and pilot sign on the tablet. Exchange, conditions, UKC table and passage log export as one PDF under your organisation's branding.

Record export (CSV)

Records export as CSV as well as the signed PDF.

Vessel memory

Finalise an exchange and the ship's particulars are kept. Next visit, type three letters of her name and around thirty fields fill themselves in.

Passage & clearance

Passage plan & charts

Build passages on the chart with bearings, distances and speed limits per leg. Shared org passages your admin maintains, plus your own personal ones.

Under-keel clearance, by leg

Controlling depth on every leg, height of tide, squat from planned speed, and net clearance against the port's margin, drawn as a depth section, not a table of numbers.

Ship & mooring

A ship diagram from the pilot card, and the mooring plan with your tugs named on station.

Weather & tides

Wind, gusts, pressure tendency, sea state and tide, resolved at the passage position rather than wherever the pilot happens to be standing, and captured onto the record.

Underway

Underway logbook

Live GPS against the plan, one-tap event presets, voice memos, and time corrections that are marked as amended rather than silently rewritten.

Built for the bridge

Day and night themes, and Apple Pencil on the chart. Designed to be read at arm's length on a wing at 0400. Native on iPad and iPhone.

Your form. Your passages. Your name on the record.

EMPX is set up around your pilotage organisation, whether that's a large provider or a solo pilot. We build your material in for you rather than leaving it as homework.

Your form

We build your organisation's MPX checklist into the app. Pilots see your questions, in your order.

Your passages

Your port's passages, waypoints, controlling depths and pre-arrival instructions, shared across the organisation.

Your fleet & chartlets

Your tugs with their bollard pull, and your berth chartlets, ready to pick from on every job.

Your name on the record

Every signed record carries your organisation's name and logo, and stays inside your organisation.

Use it on a real job. Tell us what breaks.

We're taking a small number of test pilots and pilotage organisations, solo pilots and providers both. Small on purpose: every trial is set up by hand, and we'd rather support a handful properly than a hundred badly.

  1. You apply

    Tell us who you are and where you pilot. We'll come back to you directly: no queue, no drip campaign, no sales call.

  2. We set you up

    Your organisation is provisioned by hand, with your own form and your own passages built in for you. When it's ready, you get a sign-in link by email and can use it on your next job.

  3. You run it for real

    Use it on actual pilotage, not a demo. What you report goes into the next release. Most of what shipped this month was found by using the app on a real passage.

What you get

The whole product. Nothing is held back for a paid tier: a solo pilot gets the form builder and passage builder just as an organisation does.

  • A free trial, no card. Nothing to cancel, nothing charged at the end. We agree the trial length with you.
  • Your form and your passages, set up for you rather than left as homework.
  • A direct line to the pilot who builds it, not a ticket queue.
  • Real influence on the roadmap while it is still cheap to change.

What we ask

  • Use it in service, on real exchanges. That's the only test that finds anything.
  • Tell us when it's wrong, including the small irritations. Those are usually the ones that matter on a bridge.
  • Keep the paper alongside it until you're satisfied. This is new software in a safety-critical setting and we'd think less of you if you didn't.
  • An honest verdict at the end, including "no". We'd rather hear it than not.
