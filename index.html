What We're Building

Everything a pilot needs. Nothing they don't.

EMPX is a complete digital Master Pilot Exchange platform. From the boarding ground to the berth — every checklist item, every waypoint, every signature — captured digitally, stored securely, accessible anywhere.

Full IMO Checklist

12 sections, ~80 fields, fully compliant with IMO Resolution A.960 and SOLAS Chapter V. No more paper forms.

Nautical Charts

Live chart overlays with OpenSeaMap integration. Route waypoints plotted with bearings and distances. Designed for pilotage.

Digital Signatures

Master and pilot sign directly on the tablet. Captured, locked, and exported to PDF. Tamper-proof and auditable.

Cloud Sync

Multi-tenant Firestore backend. Your org's records, routes, and waypoints — synced in real-time across every device.

Org Admin Console

Manage pilots, routes, waypoints, and billing from a dedicated admin panel. Per-org access control and subscription management.

Weather & Tides

Live maritime weather, wind conditions, and tidal data integrated directly into the pilotage workflow. No app-switching.
